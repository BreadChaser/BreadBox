# Changelog

What changed in each release, newest first. The first block lists short in-app highlights (six at most). Longer release notes follow under Details.

## Unreleased

- PlayStation controls fixed
- Updates ask first
- Updates explain delays
- Import other emulator saves

### Details

- Updates ask before installing, explain what they are waiting for, and install after you accept.
- Bring in saves made in other emulators.
- New looks: details to come.
- GBA picture options: details to come.
- Fixed: PlayStation controls keep responding in games such as Tomb Raider, including after Reset game.
- If the controls don't respond after loading an older save state, switch Controller to Analog and back to Digital once.

## v0.9.2 (public beta)

- PlayStation CHD files fixed
- Update settings open again
- Long errors fit the screen

### Details

- Fixed: PlayStation games in .chd files are found again and start.
- Settings > Updates always opens and says why updates are off.
- Long error messages now fit the screen.

## v0.9.1 (public beta)

- Safer saves on SD errors

### Details

- Fixed: an SD card read error can no longer make BreadBox lose or overwrite a save.

## v0.9.0 (public beta)

- PlayStation and GBA
- Your games in a 3D room
- Continue and save backups
- Finds games and box art
- Import your PSX3D saves
- Updates over Wi-Fi

### Details

- First public release: PlayStation and Game Boy Advance on the 3DS, in one app.
- A 3D room is the main menu, with your games on a bookcase.
- Continue, twelve postcards per game, and save backups.
- Finds your games and box art by itself; no BIOS files needed.
- Copies your saves and settings from PSX3D on first start.
- Updates itself over Wi-Fi.
- Known issues are in the [README](README.md#known-issues).
