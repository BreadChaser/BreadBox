# Room files (BBR1): asset manifest and credits

Everything in the room files is generated by the scripts in `room3d/blender/` except:

| Asset | Used for | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `breadbox/ui/hand-ref/scribbletoad-hand-lowpoly.fbx` ("Hand (low poly)", 393 quads) | the hand (both hands; the left is the same mesh mirrored at runtime), from round 3d | scribbletoad | CC BY 4.0 — credit required | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hand-low-poly-d6c802a74a174c8c805deb20186d1877 |

Changes made to the hand (CC BY 4.0 asks to indicate them): scaled to 185 mm (wrist to middle fingertip),
re-oriented into the grip frame, rigged with a Blender armature and automatic weights (each vertex bound to
its strongest bone), the thumb rotated into opposition, flat-shaded, re-UV-mapped into the BreadBox hand
texture (skin / glove tiles), a cuff, sleeve and mitten paddle added. Credit line for the app's About screen:
"Hand model: 'Hand (low poly)' by scribbletoad (Sketchfab), CC BY 4.0, modified."
The mock's Continue thumbnail is a PSX3D capture from the owner's console (see build.py).
