# Room files (BBR v3): asset manifest and credits

Room geometry and lighting are generated by `tools/room/lamplight/`.
Other artwork is credited in [Art sources](../../../tools/art/CREDITS.md).
The hand uses this upstream asset:

| Asset | Used for | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Hand (low poly)" (393 quads; upstream FBX) | the hand (both hands; the left is the same mesh mirrored at runtime) | scribbletoad | CC BY 4.0 — credit required | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hand-low-poly-d6c802a74a174c8c805deb20186d1877 |

Changes made to the hand (CC BY 4.0 asks to indicate them): scaled to 185 mm (wrist to middle fingertip),
re-oriented into the grip frame, rigged with a Blender armature and automatic weights (each vertex bound to
its strongest bone), the thumb rotated into opposition, flat-shaded, re-UV-mapped into the BreadBox hand
texture (skin / glove tiles), a cuff, sleeve and mitten paddle added. Credit line for the app's About screen:
"Hand model: 'Hand (low poly)' by scribbletoad (Sketchfab), CC BY 4.0, modified."
The upstream hand source is obtained from the linked author page; it is not
bundled as a source FBX. The shipped room is `assets/romfs/room/room.bbr`;
see `tools/room/README.md` for the generation pipeline.
